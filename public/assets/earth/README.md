`earth_day_4096.jpg` is the daylight albedo; `earth_night_4096.jpg` is the night illumination; `earth_bump_roughness_clouds_4096.jpg` packs elevation (R), roughness (G), and cloud coverage (B). The portfolio maps them onto a real-time 3D sphere and credits the author during the orbit view.
